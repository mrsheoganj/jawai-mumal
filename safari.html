Safari Information

What to Know Before Your Safari

Helpful guidelines to ensure a safe, enjoyable, and ethical wildlife experience.

📸 Photography Tips

Recommended lenses: 70-200mm or 100-400mm. Beanbags provided in every Gypsy. Flash photography is strictly prohibited.

🧥 Clothing & Weather

Neutral colors (khaki, beige, olive) are recommended. Morning drives can be chilly; warm fleeces and blankets are provided in the Gypsy.

🛡️ Safe Distances

Our drivers and trackers follow ethical wildlife viewing practices. Leopards in Jawai are habituated to vehicles and behave naturally.
